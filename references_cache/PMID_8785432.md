Considerable progress has been made towards enhancing our understanding of the 
phylogeny, ecology and biogeochemical role of dissimilatory iron-reducing 
bacteria. The known phylogenetic range of iron-reducing bacteria has expanded 
considerably, as has the known range of iron minerals that serve as a source of 
Fe(III) for anaerobic respiration. In addition, the number of biotechnological 
applications of iron-reducing bacteria, including remediation of soils and 
sediments contaminated with metals, radionuclides and organics, is rapidly 
expanding.
